About InAmigos Foundation

Founded on September 23, 2020, by Mr. Govind Shukla, InAmigos Foundation is a Section 8 registered non-profit organization based in Chhattisgarh. We are dedicated to building a more inclusive and empowered society through dedicated volunteer networks across India.

Section 8 Registered 80G & 12A Certified CSR-1 Registered NITI Aayog Listed ISO 9001:2015 Certified

Our Key Initiatives

Project Seva

Serving humanity with compassion by providing food, clothing, and essential support to underprivileged communities.

Project Bachpanshala

Nurturing young minds and building bright futures by ensuring quality education for underprivileged children.

Project Jeev

Dedicated to animal welfare, including the rescue, medical treatment, protection, and feeding of stray animals.

Project Udaan

Empowering women through skill development, financial literacy, and livelihood training programs.

Project Prakriti

Focused on environmental conservation, sustainability, and large-scale tree plantation drives for a greener tomorrow.

Project Vikas

Enhancing employability and professional skills through dedicated youth training and internship programs.
